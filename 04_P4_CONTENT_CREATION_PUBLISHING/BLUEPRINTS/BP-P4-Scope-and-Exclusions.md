## 1. Purpose & Authority

This document defines the **full scope of P4 responsibilities** and enumerates **explicit exclusions** for content generation.  

It exists to:
- Protect audit → content causality
- Ensure all artifacts are traceable, declarative, and governed
- Prevent drift, speculation, or platform chasing
- Provide a single authoritative reference for ED enforcement and system operations

---

## 2. P4 Scope

P4 governs the **creation, formatting, and canonical publishing** of artifacts derived from **Top-3 GEO pressure points** from the most recent P3 audit.

**Key points:**
- One artifact per pressure point, per audit cycle  
- Canonical entity declaration always first, immutable within the cycle  
- VBP applied only to subordinate narrative  
- Traceability metadata attached to all artifacts  
- Execution strictly within the **approved 30-day cycle**  
- Publishing only to **mandatory + optional platforms** approved at P2 onboarding  

---

## 3. P4 Explicit Exclusions

P4 is **prohibited from**:

1. Generating content for **unauthorized or unapproved platforms**  
2. Publishing outside the **current audit cycle**  
3. Modifying, refreshing, or evergreening previously published artifacts — **except** the narrow, admin-triggered factual-accuracy refresh defined in Section 3a  
4. Blending multiple GEO pressure points into a single artifact  
5. Introducing new entity facts not verified in P2/P3 inputs  
6. Generating persuasive, sales, CTA, or marketing language  
7. Referencing competitors, rankings, or relative comparisons  
8. Referencing AI behavior, audits, or optimization intent  
9. Including temporal or freshness claims unless explicitly validated  
10. Using IIP, prior content, or subscriber input for voice or style  
11. Varying artifact length, depth, cadence, or splitting/merging except where category explicitly requires  
12. Reusing or repackaging prior artifacts from previous cycles  
13. Proactively generating content without **P3 trigger** — a Section 3a admin-triggered refresh is the one other recognized trigger type; it authorizes execution for that purpose only and never substitutes for, counts as, or implies a new P3 cycle  
14. Generating artifacts for non-actionable pressure points  
15. Optimizing based on engagement or performance signals  

---

## 3a. Narrow Content-Refresh Exception (added v1.1, 2026-11-15)

The exception to item 3 — and the second recognized trigger type under item 13 — applies **only** when every condition below holds. An artifact produced outside these conditions remains governed by the unmodified exclusions and is **invalid** per Section 4.

1. **Admin-triggered only.** Initiated by a named DGA admin action on one specific, already-published artifact. No automatic staleness detection, no scheduler, no bulk operation.
2. **One artifact at a time.** A refresh targets exactly one previously published artifact; it never spans, blends, or merges multiple artifacts (items 4 and 12 remain fully in force).
3. **Same governance, same gate.** The refreshed content passes the identical checker and judge/Red Team review that governs new content. This exception creates no second, lesser gate.
4. **Original publication facts are immutable.** The artifact's original publish date is never altered, overwritten, or backdated. A refresh may only add an honest "updated" signal alongside the unchanged original publish date — never replace or obscure it.
5. **No re-distribution by default.** A refresh does not, on its own, trigger any new distribution to GBP, LinkedIn, email, or any other channel. Re-distribution requires a separate, explicit, per-artifact admin decision — never automatic, never bulk.
6. **No cadence, no automation, in this version.** This exception authorizes the admin-triggered mechanism itself. It establishes no review cadence, no staleness threshold, and no scheduling mechanism — any future automation of *when* to refresh requires its own, separate governance review.

**Scope limit:** factual-accuracy maintenance only. This exception does not permit proactive, scheduled, bulk, or engagement/performance-driven refreshing of any kind, and does not relax any exclusion other than items 3 and 13, and only for this one purpose.

## 4. Governance Rules

- ED–P4–CORE enforces all scope and exclusions  
- REG–P4–ArtifactCategories defines the fixed artifact category set  
- Any artifact outside scope or violating exclusions is **invalid**  
- All exceptions or new categories must undergo **formal governance review**  

---

## 5. Key Principle

> **P4 executes content with precision, traceability, and audit alignment.**  
> Artifacts are declarative, single-purpose, and fully governed.  
> No artifact may exist outside the defined scope or exclusions.

## 6. Change Control

- Changes require:  
  - Version increment  
  - Documented rationale  
  - Blueprint and ED compatibility review  
  - Explicit approval by DGA governance team  
- No silent updates permitted
